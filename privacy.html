Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 15, 2025

Our Commitment: The Endurance Fuel Planner is designed with privacy in mind. We believe your nutrition planning data should remain private and under your control.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how the Endurance Fuel Planner ("we," "our," or "the Tool") handles your information. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our practices.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The Endurance Fuel Planner uses your browser's local storage to save your data. This means:

  • Fuel plans: Your saved nutrition plans and product selections
  • Custom products: Any custom products you create
  • Favorites: Products you mark as favorites
  • Multi-leg configurations: Your event leg setups and fuel distributions
  • User preferences: Your target carbs, sodium, caffeine settings
Important: All this data is stored locally in your browser only. It is NOT sent to any server or shared with us or anyone else.

2.2 Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect, store, or have access to:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, address)
  • Your fuel plans or nutrition data
  • Your browsing history
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers
  • Cookies for tracking purposes

3. How We Use Information

Since all your data is stored locally on your device:

  • We cannot access, view, or use your nutrition planning data
  • Your data is used solely to provide functionality of the Tool
  • Your data remains on your device and is never transmitted to our servers

4. Data Storage and Security

4.1 Local Storage

Your data is stored in your browser's local storage using standard browser APIs. This means:

  • Data persists between sessions on the same browser and device
  • Data is isolated to your browser and not accessible to other websites
  • Clearing your browser data will delete your saved plans
  • Different browsers or devices will have separate data

4.2 Your Control Over Data

You have complete control over your data:

  • Export: Use the download feature to save your plans locally
  • Delete: Clear your browser data to remove all stored information
  • No account required: No registration means no centralized data storage

5. Third-Party Services

5.1 Currently Used Third-Party Services

The Tool uses the following third-party services:

  • Font Awesome: For icons (loaded from CDN). Review their privacy policy at fontawesome.com/privacy

5.2 Third-Party Links

The Tool may contain links to external websites (e.g., for product information or race details). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external sites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies.

6. Children's Privacy

The Endurance Fuel Planner is intended for use by adults and athletes capable of making informed nutrition decisions. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you are under 13, please do not use this Tool.

7. Data Sharing and Disclosure

Since we do not collect your data, we cannot share it. We will never:

  • Sell your information to third parties
  • Share your nutrition plans with anyone
  • Use your data for advertising purposes

The only exception would be if required by law, but since we don't collect personal data, there would be nothing to share.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data: All your data is visible in the Tool's interface
  • Delete your data: Clear your browser's local storage at any time
  • Export your data: Use the download feature to save your plans
  • Control your data: All data stays on your device; you have complete control

9. Data Retention

Your data is retained in your browser's local storage until you:

  • Manually clear your browser data
  • Uninstall your browser
  • Use a browser feature to clear site data

We do not retain any of your data on our servers because we do not collect it.

10. International Users

The Tool can be used from anywhere in the world. Since all data is stored locally on your device:

  • No data crosses international borders to our servers
  • Data protection is governed by your local browser's security
  • Your data never leaves your device

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do:

  • We will update the "Last Updated" date at the top
  • Significant changes will be communicated through a notice on the Tool
  • Your continued use of the Tool after changes constitutes acceptance

12. Security Measures

While we implement reasonable security measures for the Tool itself:

  • Your data security depends on your device and browser security
  • We recommend keeping your browser and device software updated
  • Use strong device passwords to protect your local data
  • Be cautious when using public or shared devices

13. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, please contact us using the "Report an Issue" link in the footer of the main application.

14. Consent

By using the Endurance Fuel Planner, you consent to this Privacy Policy.

Summary: The Endurance Fuel Planner is designed with privacy as a priority. All your nutrition planning data stays on your device. We don't collect, store, or have access to your personal information or fuel plans. You have complete control over your data.
