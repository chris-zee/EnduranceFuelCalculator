# Endurance Fuel Calculator

A web application to help endurance runners plan their nutrition strategy for races and long training runs.

## Features

- **Event Planning**: Set your event duration and hourly nutrition targets
- **Comprehensive Product Database**: Pre-loaded with 15+ popular running fuel products
- **Custom Products**: Add your own fuel products with nutritional information
- **Caffeine Strategy**: Optional caffeine planning with customizable intervals and dosing
- **Real-time Calculations**: 
  - Per-hour nutrition breakdown
  - Total event nutrition totals
  - Target comparisons with visual feedback
- **Smart Warnings**: Get alerts when your plan doesn't meet your nutrition targets
- **Persistent Storage**: Custom products are saved to your browser's local storage

## How to Use

1. **Set Your Event Details**:
   - Enter your event duration (in hours)
   - Set your target carbs per hour (typically 60-90g/hour)
   - Set your target sodium per hour (typically 300-700mg/hour)
   - Optionally add a caffeine strategy

2. **Build Your Fuel Plan**:
   - Click on pre-loaded products to add them to your plan
   - Adjust quantities as needed
   - Add custom products if your favorites aren't listed

3. **Review Your Plan**:
   - Check per-hour nutrition to ensure proper pacing
   - Review total event nutrition
   - Adjust based on warnings and recommendations

## Nutritional Targets

**Carbohydrates**: Most endurance athletes benefit from 60-90g of carbs per hour during events longer than 90 minutes.

**Sodium**: Sodium needs vary widely (300-700mg/hour) based on sweat rate, temperature, and individual physiology.

**Caffeine**: Research suggests 3-6mg per kg of body weight can improve performance. Timing matters - consider spreading doses throughout your event.

Simply open `index.html` in your web browser. No installation or server required!
